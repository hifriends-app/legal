2. How HiFriends Works

Core Features

  • Automatic Delivery: When you take a photo, we use face recognition to automatically deliver it to the HiFriends users who appear in it. Because the people in a photo are not always people you know, this can include HiFriends users you don’t personally know — if someone in your photo is on HiFriends, they may receive it.
  • Scan & Deliver: If you turn it on, HiFriends can scan recent photos in your camera roll, on your device, to find ones with your friends in them, and deliver those photos to them. Scan & Deliver is optional and off unless you enable it.
  • Your Feed: Receive photos of yourself taken by others.
  • Cascade Delete: Delete any photo of yourself from everywhere on HiFriends — your feed, the photographer’s HiFriends Roll (the in-app collection of photos they’ve taken), and everyone else’s feed.
  • Your Profile: Choose photos to share on your HiFriends profile. Profiles are visible to other signed-in HiFriends users — by default, only to your friends. HiFriends profiles are not published on the open web.

Friends and Friend Search

  • Friend connections on HiFriends are one-sided: anyone can add you as a friend, and you can add anyone, without the other person needing to accept.
  • HiFriends includes friend search. Your first name, last name, and username are visible to other signed-in users through search, so people can find friends and identify who took their photos.

New Features

We’re constantly improving HiFriends. New features will be added regularly.

The person you block is not notified.

6. Deletion Rights

Cascade Delete

When you delete a photo of yourself:

  • It’s removed from ALL users on HiFriends immediately
  • This includes: your feed, the photographer’s HiFriends Roll, all other recipients’ feeds
  • Also removed from: your profile page and anyone else’s profile page where it was published
  • It’s permanent and can’t be undone
  • The photographer is NOT notified
  • The photographer may notice it’s gone from their HiFriends Roll
  • If the photo also lives on the photographer’s phone — for example, a photo they took with their own camera and sent through Scan & Deliver — that copy stays on their device. HiFriends removes the photo from HiFriends. We can’t remove it from someone’s phone.

Account Deletion

When you delete your account:

  • Your account, your photos, and your face data are all deleted
  • Your face data (face encodings) is permanently destroyed right away — not on a schedule
  • Photos you took of other people are removed from HiFriends, including copies that were delivered to other users
  • Everything is gone from HiFriends immediately. Clearing the underlying stored files runs straight after and completes within 30 days.
