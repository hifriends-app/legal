1. Introduction

Welcome to HiFriends! This Privacy Policy explains how HiFriends Inc. (“we,” “us”) handles your information when you use our app.

HiFriends uses face recognition to deliver photos to the people in them. Because the people in a photo are not always the person who took it, you may receive photos taken by other HiFriends users — and photos you take may be delivered to other users — including people you don’t personally know but who are on HiFriends. Section 13 explains the consent this involves.

By using HiFriends, you agree to this policy. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the app.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name, email, username, password

Face Recognition and Biometric Data

To deliver photos to you, HiFriends uses face recognition. This section explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your rights. We apply these terms to all users, regardless of where you live.

What we collect and why. When you create your account, you provide enrollment photos. From those photos, HiFriends creates a face template — a mathematical representation of your facial geometry. We use your face template to recognize you in photos, including photos other people take, so those photos can be delivered to you. To keep recognition accurate, your face template may be refined over time, subject to quality limits, from photos other HiFriends users capture of you; all of this data carries the same protections and is destroyed the same way. We create one face template for you, and we do not use it for any purpose other than recognizing you to deliver photos to you.

Service providers. We use a third-party service provider, Amazon Web Services (Amazon Rekognition), to create and match face templates on our behalf, solely to provide HiFriends to you. Our service providers may only use your biometric data to provide the service and must keep it secure; they may not use it for their own purposes.

How long we keep it, and how it’s destroyed. We store your face template while your account is active. If you delete your account, we permanently destroy your face template right away. In all cases, we permanently destroy your face template when the purpose for collecting it has been satisfied, or within 24 months of your last interaction with HiFriends, whichever comes first.

We never sell it. We do not sell, lease, trade, or otherwise profit from your biometric data.

When we disclose it. We do not disclose, redisclose, or otherwise disseminate your biometric data except: (1) with your consent; (2) to the service providers described above, to provide HiFriends to you; (3) to complete a financial transaction you request or authorize; or (4) when required by law, or by a valid warrant or subpoena.

Security. We protect your biometric data using a reasonable standard of care, and in a manner at least as protective as the way we store and transmit other confidential and sensitive information.

Your consent and choices. We create your face template only after you provide your consent during sign-up. Because recognizing you in photos is core to how HiFriends works, this consent is required to create an account. You can have your face template permanently deleted at any time by deleting your account.

Camera Roll Access (Scan & Deliver)

If you turn on Scan & Deliver, HiFriends scans recent photos in your camera roll, on your device, to find ones with your friends in them. Detecting faces happens on your phone. To identify whether a detected face belongs to one of your friends, face images are sent to HiFriends to be matched, and are not kept afterward. Only photos where a friend is identified are uploaded and delivered to that friend. Apart from that, nothing else from your camera roll leaves your device — not your other photos, your albums, or your filenames.

Scan & Deliver is optional. You control photo-library access through your device’s settings, and you can turn it off at any time.

Photos

  • Photos you take
  • Photos you receive
  • Photos you publish to your profile
  • You can save photos you’re in to your device
  • HiFriends does not prevent or notify you of screenshots. Photos shared on any platform can be captured this way. Our cascade delete removes photos from HiFriends but cannot affect screenshots or copies already saved outside the app.

Device & Notification Information

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • A push notification token — an identifier your device provides so we can send you notifications. We collect this only if you enable notifications, and we use it only to deliver them.

Crash & Diagnostic Data

To keep HiFriends working reliably, we use Sentry, a third-party crash and diagnostics service. When the app crashes or behaves unexpectedly, Sentry receives technical information about what happened. To help us reproduce and fix problems, Sentry also records a small sample of app sessions — and a larger share of sessions where an error occurs — which can include recordings of the screens you viewed within HiFriends. Sentry may also receive your device’s IP address. We use this information only to diagnose and fix bugs, not to build a profile of you.

Background Uploads

To make sure your photos finish uploading even if you close the app or briefly lose your connection, an upload you’ve already started may continue in the background after you leave HiFriends. This happens only to complete a transfer reliably.

Basic Usage Info

  • How you use the app
  • Performance data
  • Device type and app version

Face Recognition Technology

Accuracy Notice:

  • Face recognition is not 100% accurate
  • Sometimes photos may be delivered to the wrong people (false positives)
  • Sometimes photos may not be delivered to the right people (false negatives)
  • Accuracy improves as you use the app

5. Your Control & Rights

Delete Any Photo of Yourself

Cascade Delete: Remove a photo from everywhere on HiFriends:

  • Your feed
  • The photographer’s HiFriends Roll (the in-app collection of photos they’ve taken)
  • Everyone else who received it
  • Your profile page
  • Anyone else’s profile page where it was published

How to delete: From your Me Feed, select the photo and confirm deletion. The photo is removed from everyone’s HiFriends immediately; the stored image files and face data are then permanently destroyed. No notifications are sent to the photographer.

What we can’t do: if the photo also exists somewhere outside HiFriends — on the photographer’s phone, or in a screenshot someone saved — we can’t reach it. Cascade delete removes the photo from HiFriends everywhere. It cannot remove it from someone’s device.

Delete Your Account

When you delete your account:

  • Your account, your photos, and your face data are all deleted
  • Your face data is permanently destroyed right away — not on a schedule
  • Everything is gone from HiFriends immediately; clearing the underlying stored files runs straight after and completes within 30 days

Block Anyone

You can block any HiFriends user at any time. See Blocking in Section 13.

“Not Me” Feedback

If you receive a photo that isn’t you, mark it “Not Me” to:

  • Remove it from your Me Feed
  • Help improve recognition accuracy

Request Your Data

To request a copy of your data, email support@hifriends.ai with the subject “Data Request” from your registered email address. We’ll respond within 30 days.

14. Your Privacy Rights

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives California residents specific rights over their personal information. We honor these rights for every HiFriends user, no matter where you live:

  • Know and access. Ask us what personal information we’ve collected about you and get a copy. Use the Request Your Data process in Section 5.
  • Delete. Ask us to delete your personal information. You can do most of this yourself, instantly, in the app — delete any photo of yourself, or delete your whole account. For anything else, email us.
  • Correct. Ask us to fix personal information that’s inaccurate — email us at support@hifriends.ai and we’ll correct it.
  • No sale, no sharing for ads. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because there’s no sale or sharing, there’s nothing you need to opt out of.
  • Limits on sensitive information. Your face template is sensitive personal information under California law. We use it only to provide HiFriends — recognizing you so your photos can be delivered to you — and for nothing else.
  • No retaliation. Exercising any of these rights never changes how HiFriends treats you.

How to exercise these rights: use the in-app controls described in Section 5, or email support@hifriends.ai from your registered email address. We verify requests by matching your registered email, and we respond within 30 days. You can also have an authorized agent submit a request for you; we’ll ask for proof that you gave them permission.

The categories of personal information we collect, why we collect them, and who we share them with are described in Sections 2 through 4 of this policy.
